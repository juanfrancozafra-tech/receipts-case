Clara Receipts

A free receipt-to-Excel tool, designed as an experiment to open a bottom-up acquisition channel for Clara.

▶ Live prototype ◆ GitHub repo (PRD · docs)

Who it's for

The person who suffers it — not the CFO. Travelling reps, admin assistants, junior analysts: high pain, no buying power, high upward influence.

The problem

Every trip: photograph each receipt, re-type every line into Excel, email finance. Effort, time, errors, repetition.

The opportunity

Not a feature — an acquisition wedge. Free useful tool → internal champion → Clara enters the spend conversation. Lower CAC than paid.

Hypothesis · Metrics · Decision rule

A free, Clara-branded receipt-to-Excel tool will drive ≥ 40% activation and convert ≥ 10% of activated users into a Clara lead event — at lower CAC than paid channels.

Leading metrics

  • Activation — first report shared · ≥ 40% (value)
  • Referral / lead event · ≥ 10% (growth)

Lagging metrics

  • Qualified leads sourced by the tool
  • CAC vs. other channels — the verdict
⛔ Kill switch

Kill if referral stays < 5% after ≥ 500 activated users (~8 wks). Gate & fix value first if activation < 25% after ~200 users.

Hipótesis cuantificada y falsable. Una métrica líder por capa (valor y growth); las lagging solo en rollout real. Y una regla concreta de matar/pausar.
